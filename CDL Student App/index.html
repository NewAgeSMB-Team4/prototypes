Terms of use
Version 2.1 · updated Jan 10, 2026
1. About this app

Cascade Driving Academy provides this app to its students to enrol, follow their training, sign their lesson logs and pay their fees. It is not the licensing authority; your CDL is issued by the state.

2. Your account

Your account is personal. Sign-in codes go to the phone number and email the school holds for you. Do not let anyone else sign in as you, and never let anyone else sign a lesson log on your behalf.

3. Your signature

When you sign a lesson log you are confirming you attended and that the times recorded are accurate. Your signature is stored with the record and forms part of a federal training record.

4. Theory tests

Section quizzes are practice and can be taken as often as you like. The final theory test is graded, its attempts are limited by your programme, and every attempt is recorded. Taking it for someone else, or having someone take it for you, ends your enrolment.

5. Documents you upload

Documents must be your own, current and legible. Uploading a document that is not yours is grounds for removal from the programme and may be reported.

6. Sessions, absence and fees

Sessions are booked by the office. Tell us as early as you can if you cannot attend; late notice and no-shows may carry a charge under your enrolment agreement, as may retests and retakes.

7. Your enrolment agreement wins

Where these terms and your signed enrolment agreement differ, the agreement applies. You can read yours under More.

Privacy policy
Version 2.1 · updated Jan 10, 2026
What we hold about you

Your name, date of birth, contact details and address; the documents you upload with their expiry dates; your permit and licence details; your training records, hours, test scores and signatures; your payments; and the messages between you and the office.

Why we hold it

To train you, to prove to FMCSA and the state that the training happened, and to take payment. Most of it we are required to keep.

Who we share it with

FMCSA's Training Provider Registry and your state licensing agency, which is how your training reaches your licence record. Your instructor and the office. Auditors and our insurer on request. We do not sell your information and we do not share it with employers unless you ask us to.

How long we keep it

Training records for three years after you finish or leave, as the regulation requires. Payment records as tax law requires.

Your rights

Ask the office for a copy of your file, or for a correction to your personal details. Training records, signed logs and test scores cannot be changed or deleted, because they are the evidence behind your licence.

If you withdraw

We keep what the regulation requires and stop using your details for scheduling or marketing.
