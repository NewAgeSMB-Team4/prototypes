VAT prototype — splash background
=================================

Drop a licensed photo here, named exactly:

    splash.jpg        (also accepts .jpg re-encoded; keep the name)

Recommended shot:  flatbed tow truck on a highway at golden hour / dusk,
shot roughly portrait or centre-weighted so the truck survives a 9:19.5 crop.
Warm orange light suits the VAT palette.

Size:   1170 x 2532 (iPhone 15 Pro @3x) or larger, portrait
Weight: compress to under ~400 KB — it is the first paint of the app

Behaviour:
  - present  -> the photo fills the splash, with a brand scrim over it
  - absent   -> the built-in vector dusk-highway scene is used instead
Nothing breaks either way.
