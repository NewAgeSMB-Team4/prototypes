      Terms of use
      Version 2.1 · updated Jan 10, 2026
      1. Who this applies to

      This portal is provided by Cascade Driving Academy to its instructors and assessors for recording training delivered on the school's behalf.

      2. Your account

      Your account is personal. Do not let anyone else sign in as you or record a session under your name. Sign-in codes are sent to the number the office holds for you.

      3. Accuracy of records

      Session records, hours and sign-offs you enter form part of a federal training record. Enter only what you observed, at the time you observed it. Knowingly recording training that did not happen is grounds for dismissal and may be a federal offence.

      4. Signatures

      Signing a session record is your professional certification of it. Both your signature and the student's are stored with the record and cannot be removed.

      5. Corrections

      A submitted record cannot be edited in the portal. Ask the office to make a correction; the original and the change are both kept.

      6. Availability and scheduling

      Availability you set here is an offer, not a booking. The office books sessions and may change them.

      7. Device and connectivity

      Use a device with a screen lock. If you lose the device, tell the office at once so your access can be revoked.

      Privacy policy
      Version 2.1 · updated Jan 10, 2026
      What we hold about you

      Your name, contact details, employee ID, licence and certification documents with their expiry dates, your availability, and the session records you create.

      Why we hold it

      To schedule you against students you are qualified to teach, to prove to FMCSA and the state that training was delivered by a qualified instructor, and to pay you.

      Student information you see

      You see only the students assigned to you, and only what you need: progress, documents that affect eligibility, and their session history. Do not share it outside the school.

      How long we keep it

      Training records are kept for three years as the regulation requires. Employment records follow state law.

      Who we share it with

      FMCSA's Training Provider Registry and the state licensing agency, where a record names you as the instructor. Auditors and insurers on request. Nobody else.

      Your rights

      You can ask the office for a copy of what we hold and for corrections to your own details. Session records cannot be deleted, because they are part of a student's federal record.
