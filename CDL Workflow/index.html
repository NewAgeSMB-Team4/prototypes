Watch out
Tell students to start the TSA check on day one. Hazmat also renews every five years with a fresh TSA check, which makes the refresher a repeatable product.
Why hazmat is the exception: it grants no new vehicle class, so FMCSA requires theory only. The school's work ends at the registry filing; the remaining two steps belong to TSA and the state.

The final theory test unlocks the moment the last section of that appendix is complete, whichever programme it is. Every programme uses the same machinery: one status lifecycle, one session model, one signature rule, one compliance check. What differs is the required documents, the hour targets, the number of registry filings, and whether a vehicle and a state skills test are involved at all.
