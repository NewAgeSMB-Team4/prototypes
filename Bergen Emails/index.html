Bergen Kids — Email templates

Four transactional emails · 600 px, responsive down to 320 px

One shell, four messages: the wordmark stands on the cream page with nothing boxed around it, the colour lives in the hero strip inside the card, and the footer is quiet type under it. The templates declare color-scheme: light — the wordmark is dark green on transparency, so an inverted ground would either swallow it or force a plate behind it. Apple Mail and Outlook honour that and leave the design alone.
