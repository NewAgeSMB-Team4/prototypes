Two things the system does, not a person

Nobody marks training complete, in theory, range or road. The system does it when every required section, skill or unit carries both signatures, the instructor's and the student's. Hours on an unsigned log do not count.

Nobody assigns the final theory test either. It unlocks the moment the last section is complete.

Other programmes

Class B and the B → A upgrade follow the same chart with fewer sections and lower hour targets.

Passenger and school bus follow it too, but the student already holds a CDL, so the permit step becomes an endorsement with a restriction, and the training and test happen in a bus.

Hazmat stops after the theory certification is accepted. No permit, no driving, no assessments, no skills test. The student then takes the state hazmat test and the TSA check themselves, and the office records the endorsement.
